Selected Research Projects

Reinforcement Learning · Construction

ProactiveCPM — Live-Signal RAG + RL for Construction Scheduling

A six-component framework combining a project-specific retrieval layer (LiveRAG) over five live operational signal channels (IoT sensors, supplier communications, HR systems, weather APIs) with a GNN + PPO reinforcement-learning agent to proactively manage Critical Path Method (CPM) schedules.

Evaluated on 1,500 simulated construction projects: 20.6% mean delay reduction versus static CPM (p < 0.001), 38.6% of catastrophic overruns prevented, and LLM-generated project-manager briefings in under 30 seconds versus the industry-standard 24-hour reactive lag.

Reinforcement Learning RAG GNN Construction Scheduling

Foundational Mathematics Research

Before moving into applied industrial engineering and AI, my graduate work in mathematics focused on algebraic combinatorics — specifically the algebraic invariants of edge ideals associated with circulant graph structures.

M.S. Thesis: Algebraic Invariants Associated with Bristled Cubic Circulant Graphs

Extensively explored the algebraic invariants of whisker cubic circulant graphs. Considered the associated edge ideal in the polynomial ring over a field and investigated its fundamental characteristics, including regularity, depth, Stanley depth, and projective dimension. This work led to two peer-reviewed journal publications (see Publications).
