About

I am currently pursuing an M.S. in Industrial Engineering at New Mexico State University (2025–present), building on an M.S. in Mathematics from the National University of Science and Technology (NUST), Islamabad, and a B.S. in Mathematics from the International Islamic University, Islamabad. My work sits at the intersection of applied mathematics, operations research, and machine learning: I design predict-then-optimize frameworks for procurement and inventory decisions, reinforcement-learning agents for construction scheduling, and multi-agent AI systems for fraud detection and clinical decision support.

My earlier training in algebraic combinatorics — including a thesis on algebraic invariants of bristled cubic circulant graphs — gives my applied work a rigorous mathematical foundation. I currently serve as a peer reviewer for Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence (Elsevier, IF ≈ 8.0) and as a Section Leader / Python Instructor for Stanford University's Code in Place program, teaching learners from over 100 countries.

I am actively seeking a Ph.D. position where I can continue developing quantitative, AI-driven decision systems for supply chain, manufacturing, and infrastructure applications.
